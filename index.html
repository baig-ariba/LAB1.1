Batman Begins

Batman Begins is a 2005 superhero film directed by Christopher Nolan and co-written by Nolan and David S. Goyer. It serves as a gritty reboot of the Batman film franchise, exploring the character's origin story from the murder of Bruce Wayne's parents to his training with the League of Shadows and his emergence as the vigilante Batman in Gotham City
